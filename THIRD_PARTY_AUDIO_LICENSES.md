# Third-party audio and production-tool licenses

## Audio content

All NotaNota compositions, MIDI files, MusicXML scores, and rendered waveforms in
this repository are original project assets generated by
`scripts/audio/generate-notanota-audio.mjs`.

No downloaded samples, SoundFonts, loops, AI-music outputs, or third-party audio
recordings are used or redistributed.

## FFmpeg production dependency

- Package: `ffmpeg-static` 5.3.0
- Binary: FFmpeg 6.1.1 essentials build from gyan.dev
- Repository: <https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static>
- FFmpeg legal information: <https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html>
- Effective binary license reported by the package: GNU GPL version 3
- Role: offline encoding to OGG/Opus and MP3, loudness/true-peak measurement,
  and browser-decodability validation

The FFmpeg executable is a development-only npm dependency. It is not copied
into `public/`, the GitHub Pages artifact, or any NotaNota audio file.

## SoundFonts and external renderers

MuseScore, FluidSynth, and GeneralUser GS were not used. The source MIDI and
MusicXML are rendered by NotaNota's deterministic built-in soft-piano synthesis,
so no SoundFont license applies.
